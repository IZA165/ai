Bubble Sort

Compares adjacent pairs and swaps if out of order. Repeats full passes until a pass occurs with zero swaps. Simple to code, slow on large lists.

Insertion Sort

Takes one item at a time and inserts it into its correct position in the sorted portion on the left. Very fast on nearly sorted or small lists.

Merge Sort

Divide and conquer: repeatedly splits list in half until sublists of size 1, then merges sorted pairs back together. Much faster on large datasets, uses more memory.
